01 / THE COMPANY

A foundation in design.

Inggita Aryanta Inastu is a holding company led by Indina Putri. With a foundation built on more than 30 years in interior design, the company continues to develop its creative portfolio across design, fashion and lifestyle.

Our work is guided by an appreciation for thoughtful spaces, enduring craftsmanship and the stories behind the things we create.
